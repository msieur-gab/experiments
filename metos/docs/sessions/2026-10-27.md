# 2026-10-27: from Dusk OS to metos

A condensed record of the conversation that started metos: the questions asked, the answers reached, and why. Decisions are also listed in `../decisions.md`.

## 1. Dusk OS, Forth and Gab's boards

Gab asked what Dusk OS runs on, whether it fits a Pi Zero 2W or an Orange Pi Zero 2W, and where Linux comes in.

- Dusk OS is its own 32-bit Forth OS, not Linux-based. It runs bare metal (i386, amd64, ARM, RISC-V, m68k), in usermode on a normal OS, or as PID 1 on a bare Linux kernel.
- Pi Zero 2W: plausible on bare metal through the Raspberry Pi 1–3 target (same SoC family), though not listed and last tested on an older Dusk version.
- Orange Pi Zero 2W (Allwinner H618): not supported bare metal; realistic only through Linux (usermode, or PID 1 on Armbian's kernel).
- Skills: Forth above all, a C toolchain and QEMU on a host, a serial console, and reading SoC datasheets.

Alignment with Symbiot (his Orange Pi mini-OS with phone companion): strong in spirit (permacomputing, operators not users, authorable systems), weak in practice (Symbiot needs Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, crypto, concurrency and ML, all absent from Dusk). Recommendation: keep Armbian for Symbiot; use Dusk as a reference, or as a separate offline device.

Forth explained: a stack language from ~1970 where you grow a vocabulary of words. A tiny NLU in Forth is plausible for the grammar layer and a small integer-weight classifier, not for transformers or voice. In Forth an intent can literally be a word and its slots the values on the stack. Forth also runs in browsers (WebAssembly), on phones and on any computer.

## 2. The idea: a collapse knowledge base

Gab proposed a "collapseKB": hundreds of resources on plants, vegetables and survival basics, explorable by text and intent like ptiLU/TermNL, extended by dropping in a Markdown file.

- Flat folder with tags inside each file, rather than nested folders, so entries stay self-describing when copied.
- Plain `key: value` header, easy to parse in Forth.
- Extractive and abstaining, like Brainboard: a made-up answer about a plant can kill.
- Small intent set (what is, how to, is it safe, list, uses), slot = subject + aspect.

Name: Gab suggested *xero* from Greek for knowledge; *xērós* actually means "dry". Greek words for knowledge were compared (mētis, technē, epistēmē, gnōsis, phronēsis, mnēmē) and Latin ones (scientia, cognitio, peritia, usus…). Gab chose **metos**: *mētis* + OS.

## 3. The prototype

Choices (Gab picked from options): JS offline PWA for the head start, single-file C as the target, Forth also a target; laptop first; seed topics water, wild edible plants, vegetables, fire and shelter. Built in `msieur-gab/experiments`, folder `metos/`, branch `metos`.

Safety numbers were checked against sources: CDC (boiling 1 min, 3 min above ~2000 m; bleach 2 drops per litre of 5–9 % bleach, 30 min), Eawag SODIS manual (6 h sun, 2 days if more than half cloudy), BfR and AGES (wild garlic lookalikes), food safety agencies (kidney beans: soak, boil hard 10+ min).

## 4. Running anywhere

Gab asked how to make it self-executable on any hardware, from a USB stick or a cheap board like an ESP32. Answer: nothing is truly dependency-free, so stack layers (files themselves, browser page, portable binary, ESP32 beacon, bootable/Forth). See `../roadmap.md`.

## 5. Pictures

Gab asked whether SVG is portable and what Forth would do with it. SVG is text but needs a big renderer; Forth would show the caption, or a small SVG subset could be drawn.

Gab then proposed a pipeline that turns a picture into code any device can read, and insisted the result must be recognisable, not abstract: drop a photo of a dandelion, see a dandelion in the card. The `pic` block came out of this, tested on his dandelion picture with good results from 48 px wide. See `../image-format.md`.

## 6. Writing entries by hand

Uploading files was the only way to add knowledge. Gab asked for a template to fill in. Built: a guided editor (new and edit), a "try a question" findability check, saving to the kb folder on desktop Chrome, and `kb/_template.md`.

## 7. Font, memory, packaging

Gab chose Departure Mono as the foundation typeface; it is now used everywhere. He asked to store the discussion in the repo for future Claude instances (this file, `../CLAUDE.md`). Next step he set: package metos into a self-running application on a USB stick and see how it works.
