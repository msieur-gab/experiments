# metos vocabulary
# Everything metos understands about intent lives in this file.
# Edit it like a KB entry: one line per word list, comma-separated phrases.
#
#   intent NAME: phrases that signal what the person wants to do
#   aspect NAME: phrases that point to a section (## NAME) inside an entry
#   tag NAME:    other words that mean this tag
#   stop:        words ignored when matching subjects
#
# Phrases are matched as whole words, in order. Longer phrases win.

aspect identify: identify, recognise, recognize, look like, looks like, tell apart, difference, lookalike, spot
aspect eat: eat, eating, edible, taste, salad, raw, soup
aspect cook: cook, cooking, prepare, recipe
aspect grow: grow, growing, plant out, sow, cultivate, garden
aspect harvest: harvest, pick, gather, collect, when to pick
aspect store: store, storing, storage, keep, preserve
aspect safety: warning, careful, avoid
aspect use: use, uses, useful, good for, what can i do with
aspect symptoms: symptom, symptoms, signs, sick, ill

tag water: water, drink, drinking, thirst
tag food: food, eat, edible, hungry

tag fire: fire, fires
