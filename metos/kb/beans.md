title: Beans
aka: bean, dry beans, dried beans, kidney beans, runner beans, bush beans, haricot
tags: plant vegetable food garden protein
warn: Raw or undercooked dry beans are toxic, especially red kidney beans. As few as four or five raw beans can make you very ill. Soak, then boil hard for at least 10 minutes.
see: sowing-calendar, seed-saving
source: Hong Kong Centre for Food Safety on phytohaemagglutinin; US FDA guidance on red kidney beans; gardening part compiled from general references, verify before relying on it

Beans give protein, store dry for years, and improve the soil they grow in. Their seeds are also the easiest to save.

## grow
Sow outdoors after the last frost, when the soil is warm (in Central Europe from mid May). Bush beans: 5 cm apart in rows. Runner beans: at the foot of poles or strings 2 m high. Sow 3 to 4 cm deep.

## harvest
Green beans: pick young and often, which makes the plant produce more. Dry beans: leave the pods on the plant until they are dry and rattle, then shell them.

## cook
1. Soak dry beans in plenty of water for at least 5 hours, ideally overnight.
2. Throw away the soaking water.
3. Boil vigorously in fresh water for at least 10 minutes. Some authorities recommend 30 minutes.
4. Then simmer until soft.

Never cook dry beans only in a slow cooker or at low heat: lukewarm heat makes the toxin worse.

## store
Dry shelled beans completely, until they shatter when hit with a hammer. Store in airtight jars in a dry, cool place.
