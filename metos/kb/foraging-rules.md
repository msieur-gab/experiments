A few rules prevent most foraging accidents.

## how
1. Be 100 percent sure of the identification. Check several features, not one.
2. Learn the deadly plants of your area first.
3. Pick leaf by leaf. Do not pull up handfuls.
4. Avoid roadsides, sprayed fields, polluted water and areas used by dogs.
5. Start with a small amount of any new plant, and wait a day.
6. Take only part of a patch so it regrows.
7. Wash everything you pick.

## safety
Edibility tests that rely on tasting are not reliable for deadly plants: a single taste can be enough to poison. Avoid all wild mushrooms and all wild carrot-family plants unless an expert has shown you in person.
