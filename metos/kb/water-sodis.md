title: Solar water disinfection (SODIS)
aka: sodis, solar disinfection, sun water, bottle in the sun, disinfect water without fire
tags: water treatment
warn: Only works with clear water, in strong sunlight, in clear PET or glass bottles. Does not remove chemicals. Do not rely on it during continuous rain.
see: water-boil, water-filter
source: Eawag / SODIS manual (2016); SODIS fact sheets (sodis.ch)

Sunlight kills germs in clear water through UV light and heat. It needs no fuel and no chemicals, only clear bottles and sun.

## how
1. Use clean, clear, unscratched PET plastic or glass bottles of up to 2 litres. Remove labels.
2. Check the water is clear: put the filled bottle on a printed page and look down through it. If you cannot read large letters, filter or settle the water first.
3. Fill the bottle three quarters full, close it and shake for 20 seconds to add air.
4. Fill it completely and close it.
5. Lay it in direct sun, ideally on a metal roof or reflective surface, for at least 6 hours including midday.
6. If more than half the sky is cloudy, leave it for 2 full days.
7. Drink directly from the bottle, or pour into a clean cup, to avoid recontaminating it.

## safety
SODIS does not work in continuous rain, on cloudy water, or in coloured or old scratched bottles. It kills germs but does not remove chemicals or toxins.
