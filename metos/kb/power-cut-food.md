Keep the fridge and freezer doors shut. A closed fridge keeps food cold for about 4 hours. A full freezer holds for about 48 hours, a half-full one for about 24 hours.

## how
1. Keep the fridge and freezer doors closed as much as you can. Every opening lets the cold out.
2. If the power has been off for 4 hours, move meat, fish, eggs, milk and leftovers from the fridge into a cool box with ice, frozen water bottles or freezer packs.
3. In the freezer, keep food packed close together so it stays cold longer.
4. When the power comes back, check each item. Food at 4 °C (40 °F) or colder is fine to keep.
5. Frozen food that still has ice crystals, or is at 4 °C or colder, can be refrozen or cooked. The quality may be lower, but it is safe.
6. Throw away perishable food (meat, poultry, fish, eggs, leftovers) that has been above 4 °C for more than 2 hours.
7. Throw away anything that smells odd, has an odd colour or texture, or feels warm.

## safety
Food can look and smell normal and still make you ill. Tasting does not tell you whether it is safe. Before a power cut, keep a thermometer in the fridge (4 °C or colder) and in the freezer (-18 °C, 0 °F, or colder) so you can check afterwards. Keep frozen water bottles in the freezer: they keep it cold and give you drinking water later.
