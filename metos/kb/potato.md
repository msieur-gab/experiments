title: Potato
aka: potatoes, pomme de terre, kartoffel, seed potato
tags: plant vegetable food garden
warn: Green skin, sprouts, leaves and the small green berries are toxic. Cut away green parts and eyes, and discard very green potatoes.
see: sowing-calendar, seed-saving
source: compiled from general gardening references, verify before relying on it

One of the most productive staple crops for a small garden. Easy to grow and stores for months.

## grow
1. Two to four weeks before planting, set seed potatoes in a light, cool place so they grow short, sturdy sprouts.
2. Plant from April to early May in Central Europe, when the soil is no longer frozen.
3. Plant about 10 cm deep, 30 to 40 cm apart, in rows about 70 cm apart.
4. When shoots reach about 20 cm, pile soil around them, leaving only the tops visible. Repeat once or twice. Tubers exposed to light turn green.
5. Water in dry spells, especially while flowering.

## harvest
Early potatoes: dig from when the plants flower. Main crop: when the leaves have turned yellow and died back, wait about two weeks so the skins harden, then dig on a dry day.

## store
Dry them briefly in the shade, then store in the dark in a cool, frost-free place, ideally 4 to 8 °C. Light turns them green. Keep away from apples, which make them sprout.

## cook
Always cook potatoes. Boil, bake or roast.
