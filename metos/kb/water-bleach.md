title: Disinfecting water with bleach
aka: bleach, chlorine, chlorinate water, disinfect water with bleach
tags: water treatment
warn: Use only plain unscented household bleach. Never use scented, colour-safe or "with added cleaners" bleach. Never mix bleach with ammonia or other cleaners.
see: water-boil, water-filter
source: CDC, Making water safe in an emergency (cdc.gov/water-emergency)

When you cannot boil, plain household chlorine bleach kills most bacteria and viruses. It is less reliable than boiling against some parasites.

## how
For bleach with 5 to 9 percent sodium hypochlorite (most household bleach):
- 1 litre of clear water: 2 drops
- 4 litres (1 gallon) of clear water: 8 drops
- 20 litres (5 gallons) of clear water: 40 drops

If the water is cloudy, settle or strain it first. If it stays cloudy, double the dose.

For weak bleach with 1 percent sodium hypochlorite: 10 drops per litre of clear water.

1. Add the drops.
2. Stir or shake well.
3. Wait at least 30 minutes before drinking.

## safety
Read the label to know the strength. Old bleach loses strength over time. Bleach does not remove chemicals, fuel or heavy metals. Keep bleach away from children.

## store
Store treated water in clean containers with lids, out of sunlight.
