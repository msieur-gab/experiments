Honey may be used to relieve cough in adults and children at least 1 year old. One to two teaspoons can be taken directly or stirred into a warm (not hot) beverage.

## how
Give one to two teaspoons of honey directly by mouth, or stir it into a warm (not hot) drink. The CDC recommends this as a home measure for cough associated with the common cold.

## evidence
CDC lists honey among recommended home measures for easing cough and sore throat associated with the common cold; it is one of several non-medication strategies mentioned alongside rest, fluids, and humidifier use.

## safety
Never give honey to infants under 1 year old — it can contain Clostridium botulinum spores that cause infant botulism, a rare but serious illness. This warning applies regardless of honey type or brand.
