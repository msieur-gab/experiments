title: Dandelion
aka: dandelions, taraxacum, pissenlit, lowenzahn
tags: plant wild food
warn: Avoid lawns, parks and roadsides that may be sprayed or polluted.
see: foraging-rules
source: compiled from general foraging references, verify before relying on it

Almost every part of the dandelion is edible. It grows nearly everywhere and is available most of the year.

## identify
A flat rosette of leaves with deep, backward-pointing teeth, growing from a single root. Each yellow flower sits alone on a hollow, leafless stem. Broken stems and leaves release white milky sap. The seed head is a round white "clock".

Similar plants such as cat's-ear and hawkbit have hairier leaves or branched stems. They are not dangerous.

## eat
Young leaves are best raw in salad. Older leaves are bitter: boil them and discard the water. Flowers are edible raw, in batter or as tea. Roots can be cooked like a vegetable.

## harvest
Leaves are mildest in early spring and late autumn. Pick flowers in full sun when open. Dig roots in autumn.

## use
Roasted, ground roots make a coffee substitute.
