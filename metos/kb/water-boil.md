title: Boiling water
aka: boil water, boiling, make water safe, purify water, disinfect water
tags: water treatment
warn: Boiling does not remove fuel, chemicals, heavy metals or toxins. Do not use water you suspect is chemically polluted.
see: water-bleach, water-sodis, water-filter
source: CDC, Making water safe in an emergency (cdc.gov/water-emergency)

Boiling is the most reliable way to make water safe to drink. It kills bacteria, viruses and parasites.

## how
1. If the water is cloudy, let it settle or strain it through clean cloth first (see water-filter).
2. Bring the water to a rolling boil, with big bubbles that keep coming even when stirred.
3. Keep it boiling for 1 minute. Above about 2000 m altitude, boil for 3 minutes.
4. Let it cool with a lid on.
5. Store it in clean containers with lids.

## safety
Boiling kills germs only. Water contaminated by fuel, toxic chemicals or radioactive material cannot be made safe by boiling, by bleach or by filtering. Find another source.

## use
Boiled water is safe for drinking, cooking, brushing teeth, washing wounds and preparing baby formula. Pouring it between two clean containers a few times improves the flat taste.
