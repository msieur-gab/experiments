The key date is the last frost, usually around mid May in Central Europe. Tender plants go outside only after it.

## how
February to March, indoors on a bright windowsill: tomatoes, peppers, leeks.

March to April, outdoors: peas, broad beans, onions, radishes, spinach, carrots, lettuce.

April to early May, outdoors: potatoes, beetroot, chard, more lettuce and radishes.

After the last frost, mid May onward: beans, courgettes, squash, cucumbers, maize. Plant out tomatoes and peppers.

June to July: sow kale, leeks and late carrots for autumn and winter.

August to September: winter spinach, lamb's lettuce, winter radish.

October: plant garlic and sow broad beans in mild areas.
