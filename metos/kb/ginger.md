Ginger is traditionally and commonly used for nausea and vomiting, indigestion, menstrual cramps, and osteoarthritis.

## evidence
Research shows ginger may be helpful for nausea and vomiting associated with pregnancy. Studies suggest ginger dietary supplements might be helpful for reducing the severity of menstrual cramps and for knee osteoarthritis symptoms; effectiveness for chemotherapy- or post-surgery-related nausea remains uncertain.

## safety
Side effects when taken orally include abdominal discomfort, heartburn, diarrhea, and mouth and throat irritation. Possible interactions with medications exist; consult a healthcare provider before use, especially during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
