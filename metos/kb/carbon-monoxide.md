Carbon monoxide is an invisible, odourless gas from anything that burns. In a closed space it builds up quickly.

## symptoms
Headache, dizziness, weakness, nausea, confusion, sleepiness. Several people feeling ill at once in the same space is a strong sign.

## how
If you suspect it: get everyone into fresh air immediately, open everything, and turn off or put out the source. Seek medical help. Do not go back in until the space is aired out.

## safety
Cook and keep fires outside or under an open roof. Never bring a grill or charcoal inside to keep warm, even after the flames have gone out.
