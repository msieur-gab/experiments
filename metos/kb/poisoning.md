Stay calm and act fast. Check that the person is awake and breathing normally. Do not make them vomit. Call the poison centre, which tells you exactly what to do for that substance.

## how
1. Protect yourself first. Wear gloves if the substance is on their skin or mouth.
2. Check: is the person awake, answering, breathing normally? If not, call 112.
3. Unconscious but breathing normally: shout for help and put them in the recovery position. Not breathing normally: start CPR.
4. Awake and breathing: call the poison centre. In Munich: 089 19240. Other German poison centres: Berlin 030 19240, Bonn 0228 19240, Freiburg 0761 19240, Göttingen 0551 19240, Mainz 06131 19240, Erfurt 0361 730730.
5. Have the packaging, label, leftover pills, or a piece of the plant, berry or mushroom ready. The UFI code on a product label helps them identify it.
6. Wipe any leftover substance out of the mouth.
7. Give only what the poison centre tells you. As a guide: plants or mushrooms, half a glass of still water or tea; acids or alkalis such as drain cleaner, one or two glasses of water or tea; foaming products like washing-up liquid, nothing more to drink.
8. Keep any vomit and the leftovers, and give them to the ambulance crew.

## safety
Never make someone vomit. It can injure the throat and cause serious complications. Do not give milk, salt water or fizzy drinks. Give activated charcoal only if the poison centre or ambulance tells you to. A swallowed button battery is always an emergency: call 112 and get the person, usually a child, to hospital straight away, even if they seem fine. With corrosive substances, do not give mouth-to-mouth breaths; protect yourself. If in doubt, call the poison centre: calling once too often is better than once too few.
